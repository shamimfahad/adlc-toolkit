## Things that surprised me

- The test's engine `$` has no `state` noun (`$.state.get` is undefined), so tests cannot read a mod's atoms directly. The mod exposes them through a `readout` Pane instead.
- P4: the refusal also applies to an update that is started but not awaited. The write's continuation still counts as "during ui.render dispatch", and the error goes to a promise nobody catches. A timer hop (`$.clock.after`) gets past it.
- P6: inline `plugins` in `test()` load beneath the plugin under test.
- P2: a band tree taller than `maxRows` is not refused, and `$.ui.scroll(...)` on the engine `$` lets a test act as the person's wheel.
- Stubs for calls on `$` (`ui.open`, `ui.close`) must answer `{ value }`. A `ui.close` stub that returns nothing fails the test with "returned no result".
